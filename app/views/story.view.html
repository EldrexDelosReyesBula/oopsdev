Origin Story

The Stories That Started It All

We didn't have to look far to find the problem we wanted to solve. We found it right in front of us, tucked away in the daily grind of two very different workplaces.

Municipal Office

Our first encounter with the chaos of paper happened inside a Municipal Office. The task seemed simple on paper: manually transfer information from hundreds of old, thick logbooks into new handwritten ledgers. But the reality was exhausting.

It was tedious, incredibly slow, and highly prone to errors—a single misplaced stroke meant starting the entire page over. We watched weeks of valuable time slip away doing work that could have been reduced to mere days with the right digital tools. It was there, surrounded by towering stacks of paper, that we had our first realization:

“There has to be a better way.”

Electronics Shop

Not long after, we saw this same struggle from a different perspective inside a local Electronics Shop. Here, the challenge was preparing the annual profit report by reading through approximately 50 handwritten record books of daily sales.

It took days just to compile the numbers, and the risk of calculation errors loomed over every page. Because the data was physically buried in paper, the owner couldn't make quick, confident business decisions. We realized then that:

“Business owners need instant access to their own data, not hours of manual searching.”

Three Undeniable Truths

These two experiences taught us three undeniable truths:

1

Manual processes waste time and money: repetitive physical record-keeping consumes hours that could be spent growing a business.

2

Errors are inevitable when humans do repetitive handwriting and calculations without validation.

3

Fear of data loss: But the most important lesson was the third one: business owners and administrators hold on to their manual systems not because they love paper, but because they are terrified of losing decades of irreplaceable historical data.

The Realization

That is when everything clicked for us. We looked at the messy reality of manual work and realized a new truth:

“We do not just need to digitalize. We need to rescue.”
