About Our Mission

We Fix Your Oops.

Oops.dev is a student-founded startup born from a Technopreneurship project under the Associate in Information Systems (AIS) program. We help Philippine MSMEs and local government offices escape paper chaos without losing the records that matter to them.

“Oops” acknowledges that many businesses did not plan to get stuck in paper chaos — it just happened over time. “.dev” represents our commitment to using technology to solve real-world problems.

What We Do

Our Services

Business Process Rescue, Digitalization, and Custom System Development designed for Philippine MSMEs and local government offices.

Business Process Rescue

We analyze current workflows, identify bottlenecks, and design better, faster processes.

Document Digitalization

We scan, encode, and organize handwritten records, old logbooks, receipts, and files.

Custom System Development

We build tailored inventory, sales, HR, or reporting systems that fit each client's unique needs.

Data Preservation

We ensure legacy data is safely stored, backed up, and easy to access. Nothing is ever lost.

Training and Support

We teach teams how to use their new systems confidently. No tech skills required.

Our Mission

Why We Exist

To rescue Philippine MSMEs and government offices from slow, manual processes by providing customized digitalization services that modernize operations while preserving the integrity and accessibility of historical data.

Our Vision

Where We Are Going

To become the leading Business Process Rescue partner in the Philippines — where no business is left behind in the digital age, and no handwritten record is ever lost or abandoned.
